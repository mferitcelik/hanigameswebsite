Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 27, 2026

Hani Games (“Hani Games”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the information associated with your use of SoliMerge (“the Game”). This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use SoliMerge, how that information may be used, and the choices available to you.

1. Information We Collect

SoliMerge does not require users to create an account or directly provide personal information such as their name, postal address, or phone number in order to play the Game.

However, certain information may be collected automatically by the Game or by third-party services integrated into the Game. This information may include:

  • Device identifiers, including advertising identifiers where permitted
  • IP address
  • Approximate location derived from IP address
  • Device type, manufacturer, model, operating system, and operating system version
  • Application version and technical information
  • Language and regional settings
  • Advertising interaction information, such as ad impressions, views, clicks, and related events
  • App usage information where collected by our advertising or service providers
  • Diagnostic, security, fraud-prevention, and technical information

The exact information collected may vary depending on your device, operating system, location, privacy settings, and consent choices.

2. Advertising

SoliMerge may display advertisements, including rewarded advertisements.

We use third-party advertising and mediation services, including:

  • Unity LevelPlay
  • Unity Ads

These services may collect or process device information, advertising identifiers, IP addresses, approximate location, advertising interactions, application usage information, and other technical information in order to:

  • Display and deliver advertisements
  • Measure advertising performance
  • Provide personalized or contextual advertising where permitted
  • Perform analytics
  • Prevent fraud and abuse
  • Maintain security and regulatory compliance
  • Improve advertising services

Depending on your country or region, personalized advertising may only be used after obtaining any consent required by applicable law.

You may also be able to limit personalized advertising through your device privacy settings or through privacy controls made available by our advertising partners.

Unity's handling of personal data is governed by its own privacy policies and privacy controls.

3. How We Use Information

Information collected through SoliMerge may be used to:

  • Operate and maintain the Game
  • Provide gameplay-related functionality
  • Deliver advertisements and rewarded advertising
  • Measure and improve advertising performance
  • Understand technical performance and usage
  • Detect, prevent, and investigate fraud, abuse, or security incidents
  • Comply with legal and regulatory requirements

We do not sell personal information directly to third parties. However, advertising providers may process or share certain device and advertising-related information as described in their respective privacy policies and as permitted by applicable law.

4. Third-Party Services

SoliMerge uses third-party services that may independently collect and process information. These services currently include Unity LevelPlay and Unity Ads.

Third-party providers process information according to their own privacy policies, terms, and applicable legal obligations. Hani Games does not control the privacy practices of third-party service providers.

5. Advertising Identifiers and Tracking

Depending on your device, region, operating system, and consent choices, advertising services may use identifiers such as the Android Advertising ID or Apple's Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA).

Where required by applicable law or platform rules, permission or consent will be requested before information is used for personalized advertising or tracking. You may change advertising and privacy preferences through your device settings.

6. Data Sharing

Information may be shared with service providers when necessary to operate the Game and provide services such as advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, security, and regulatory compliance.

These service providers may process information on our behalf or according to their own privacy policies. We may also disclose information where required by law, legal process, regulatory obligation, or where reasonably necessary to protect users, Hani Games, or others from fraud, abuse, or security threats.

7. Data Retention

Hani Games retains information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law.

Third-party service providers may maintain their own data retention practices. Their retention periods are governed by their respective privacy policies.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable administrative and technical measures designed to protect information associated with the use of SoliMerge.

Information transmitted by third-party SDKs may be protected using encryption in transit where supported by those services. No electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including the right to:

  • Request access to certain personal information
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of certain personal information
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent
  • Opt out of certain personalized advertising or tracking

Requests relating directly to Hani Games may be submitted using the contact information below. Information processed independently by third-party advertising providers may also need to be managed through the privacy controls offered by those providers.

10. Children's Privacy

SoliMerge is not designed or directed specifically toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13.

If we become aware that personal information from a child has been collected in a manner inconsistent with applicable law, we will take appropriate steps to delete or otherwise handle the information as required.

11. International Data Processing

Third-party service providers used by SoliMerge may process information in countries other than the country in which you reside. Where required, appropriate safeguards are used in accordance with applicable data protection laws.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to SoliMerge, our service providers, legal requirements, or privacy practices.

When the policy is updated, the effective date at the top of this page will also be updated. We encourage users to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
