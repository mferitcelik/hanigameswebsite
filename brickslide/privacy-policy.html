Brick Slide Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Hani Games ("Hani Games", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use Brick Slide (the "Game").

1. Information We Process

Depending on your device, region, settings, and consent choices, the Game and our service providers may process:

  • Device and technical information, such as device model, operating system, language, app version, IP address, network information, and diagnostic data.
  • Advertising and attribution information, such as advertising identifiers where permitted, ad impressions, ad interactions, ad revenue information, campaign attribution, and consent status.
  • Gameplay and analytics information, such as session activity, level progress, game events, feature usage, and performance information.
  • Purchase-related information supplied by the relevant app store. We do not receive full payment-card details.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to operate and improve the Game, understand performance and gameplay, measure advertising and acquisition, prevent fraud and abuse, comply with legal obligations, and provide advertising in accordance with your consent choices and applicable law.

3. Advertising, Analytics, and Attribution Partners

Brick Slide uses third-party services for advertising mediation, analytics, attribution, and related measurement. These services may process information under their own privacy policies and, where required, subject to your consent choices.

  • AppLovin MAX for advertising mediation and consent-related ad flows.
  • Google / AdMob and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) for advertising and consent management where applicable.
  • Tenjin for mobile measurement and attribution.
  • GameAnalytics for game analytics.
  • Google Firebase Analytics for analytics and advertising-impression measurement.
  • Meta technologies where integrated for advertising measurement and related services.
  • Other advertising networks made available through AppLovin MAX mediation. The specific partners available to a user can vary by region and ad availability.

In regions where consent is required, the Game may present a consent-management interface that identifies relevant advertising partners and allows you to make or update applicable choices.

4. App Tracking Transparency on iOS

On supported Apple devices, the Game may request permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework before accessing data that may be used to track you across apps and websites owned by other companies. You can change this permission later in your device settings.

5. Legal Bases and Regional Privacy Choices

Where required by law, we rely on consent for personalized advertising, tracking, or similar processing. We may also process information where necessary to provide the Game, pursue legitimate interests such as security and product improvement, or comply with legal obligations. Privacy rights vary by jurisdiction and may include rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to certain processing.

6. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, including security, analytics, legal, accounting, and fraud-prevention purposes. Our service providers may maintain information according to their own retention policies.

7. Data Sharing and International Transfers

Information may be shared with service providers and advertising, analytics, attribution, platform, and compliance partners that help operate the Game. These providers may process information in countries other than your own, subject to applicable legal safeguards.

8. Children

Brick Slide is not directed to children under the minimum age required for independent consent to data processing in their jurisdiction. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

9. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures designed to protect information. No method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version on this page and revise the "Last updated" date.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or requests concerning Brick Slide, please contact Hani Games through our official website at hanigames.com.

This policy should be read together with any consent choices shown inside the Game and the privacy information provided by the relevant third-party services.
